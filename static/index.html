Label guide

Evidence class

  • ✔ Confirmed An operator confirmed it, backed by a primary source or two independent publishers.
  • ◐ Attributed Reported by a named source, not yet confirmed.
  • ⚠ Unverified Machine-collected item awaiting review. Never treat it as fact.

Severity (operator-set)

  • ▲▲▲▲ Critical Safety-relevant or nationwide halt of critical infrastructure.
  • ▲▲▲ High Nationwide or multi-sector loss of a widely used service for hours.
  • ▲▲ Moderate Broad but partial or degraded service.
  • ▲ Low Limited users, components or regions.
  • ? Unknown Not enough evidence to judge.

Confidence (computed)

  • ●●● High Confirmed with primary or independent evidence, and recent.
  • ●●○ Medium Primary source or two independent publishers, not yet confirmed, or evidence aging.
  • ●○○ Low Single source, crowd reports or forum items only.
